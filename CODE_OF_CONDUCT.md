# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We keep Sandvoxel a calm place to make things. Everyone who contributes, plays, reports bugs, or writes docs is owed a harassment-free experience, regardless of age, body size, caste, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient. People are learning at different speeds.
- Assume good faith in issues and reviews.
- Give credit for ideas, art, and code.
- Accept constructive criticism gracefully, and offer it the same way.
- Respect that this is a cozy project: no spam, no drama, no gatekeeping.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behaviour may be reported to the maintainers through the repository owner's GitHub profile. Reports are kept confidential. Maintainers may remove harmful content, warn participants, or ban people from project spaces, in proportion to what happened.
